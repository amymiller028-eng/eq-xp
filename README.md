# EQ XP

A short, browser-based simulation for practicing the four emotional intelligence skills: Self-Awareness, Self-Management, Social Awareness and Relationship Management.

**Scenario 1: The Board Deck.** Your boss, Dana, floods your chat at the end of the day about a project she needs for the board. The next morning you walk to her office for a hard conversation, with colleagues to talk to (or be interrupted by) along the way.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or use the GitHub Pages link for this repo.

## Adding art

The game loads character art from the `art` folder:

- `avatar-1.png`, `avatar-2.png`, … are the player looks the "Randomize avatar" button shuffles through
- `dana-face.png`, `gloria-face.png`, `jordan-face.png`, `priya-face.png` are the supporting cast
- `dana-cutout.png` is Dana on a transparent background, for the office conversation
- optional `scene-laptop.png`, `scene-serious.png`, `scene-talk.png`, `scene-listen.png`, `scene-smile.png`, `scene-wide.png` replace the conversation view with full scene art
